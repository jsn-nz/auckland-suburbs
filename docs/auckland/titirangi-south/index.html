Quick answers

What is the population of Titirangi South?

Titirangi South had 3,798 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 5.7% from 4,026 in 2018.

What is the median income in Titirangi South?

The median personal income in Titirangi South is $54,000 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $44,800 across Auckland.

How much is rent in Titirangi South?

Households renting in Titirangi South paid a median of $600 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $668 a week.

What is the median age in Titirangi South?

The median age in Titirangi South is 45.5 years (2023 Census).
