Quick answers

What is the population of Bayview East (Auckland)?

Bayview East (Auckland) had 3,732 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 3.2% from 3,618 in 2018.

What is the median income in Bayview East (Auckland)?

The median personal income in Bayview East (Auckland) is $50,600 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $44,800 across Auckland.

How much is rent in Bayview East (Auckland)?

Households renting in Bayview East (Auckland) paid a median of $590 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $685 a week.

What is the median age in Bayview East (Auckland)?

The median age in Bayview East (Auckland) is 34.1 years (2023 Census).
