Quick answers

What is the population of Herne Bay?

Herne Bay had 2,775 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 8.6% from 3,036 in 2018.

What is the median income in Herne Bay?

The median personal income in Herne Bay is $70,200 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $44,800 across Auckland.

How much is rent in Herne Bay?

Households renting in Herne Bay paid a median of $600 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $755 a week.

What is the median age in Herne Bay?

The median age in Herne Bay is 47.6 years (2023 Census).
