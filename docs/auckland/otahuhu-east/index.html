Ōtāhuhu East population and demographics

Ōtāhuhu East is a statistical area in Māngere-Ōtāhuhu Local Board, Auckland. At the 2023 Census it had 2,304 usually resident people (-0.1% since 2018). It is part of the wider Ōtāhuhu area.
