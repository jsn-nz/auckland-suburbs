Quick answers

What is the population of Donegal Park West?

Donegal Park West had 2,652 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 1.7% from 2,607 in 2018.

What is the median income in Donegal Park West?

The median personal income in Donegal Park West is $35,500 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $44,800 across Auckland.

How much is rent in Donegal Park West?

Households renting in Donegal Park West paid a median of $590 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $620 a week.

What is the median age in Donegal Park West?

The median age in Donegal Park West is 36.4 years (2023 Census).
