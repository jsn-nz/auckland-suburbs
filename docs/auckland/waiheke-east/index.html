Quick answers

What is the population of Waiheke East?

Waiheke East had 1,092 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 2.0% from 1,071 in 2018.

What is the median income in Waiheke East?

The median personal income in Waiheke East is $39,200 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $44,800 across Auckland.

How much is rent in Waiheke East?

Households renting in Waiheke East paid a median of $500 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Waiheke East?

The median age in Waiheke East is 51.6 years (2023 Census).
