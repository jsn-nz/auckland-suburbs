Quick answers

What is the population of Remuera Waiata?

Remuera Waiata had 2,865 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 2.2% from 2,802 in 2018.

What is the median income in Remuera Waiata?

The median personal income in Remuera Waiata is $59,300 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $44,800 across Auckland.

How much is rent in Remuera Waiata?

Households renting in Remuera Waiata paid a median of $796 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $825 a week.

What is the median age in Remuera Waiata?

The median age in Remuera Waiata is 47.0 years (2023 Census).
