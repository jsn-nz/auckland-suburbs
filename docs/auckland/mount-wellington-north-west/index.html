Quick answers

What is the population of Mount Wellington North West?

Mount Wellington North West had 2,970 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 5.1% from 3,129 in 2018.

What is the median income in Mount Wellington North West?

The median personal income in Mount Wellington North West is $42,900 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $44,800 across Auckland.

How much is rent in Mount Wellington North West?

Households renting in Mount Wellington North West paid a median of $520 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $600 a week.

What is the median age in Mount Wellington North West?

The median age in Mount Wellington North West is 42.8 years (2023 Census).
