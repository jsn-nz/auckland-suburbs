Quick answers

What is the population of Titirangi East?

Titirangi East had 3,243 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 2.1% from 3,177 in 2018.

What is the median income in Titirangi East?

The median personal income in Titirangi East is $51,100 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $44,800 across Auckland.

How much is rent in Titirangi East?

Households renting in Titirangi East paid a median of $575 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $678 a week.

What is the median age in Titirangi East?

The median age in Titirangi East is 39.0 years (2023 Census).
