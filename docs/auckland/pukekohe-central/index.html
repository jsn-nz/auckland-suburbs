Quick answers

What is the population of Pukekohe Central?

Pukekohe Central had 318 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 14.5% from 372 in 2018.

What is the median income in Pukekohe Central?

The median personal income in Pukekohe Central is $36,800 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $44,800 across Auckland.

How much is rent in Pukekohe Central?

Households renting in Pukekohe Central paid a median of $400 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Pukekohe Central?

The median age in Pukekohe Central is 39.9 years (2023 Census).
