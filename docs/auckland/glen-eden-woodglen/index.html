Glen Eden Woodglen population and demographics

Glen Eden Woodglen is a statistical area in Waitākere Ranges Local Board, Auckland. At the 2023 Census it had 3,231 usually resident people (+9.1% since 2018). It is part of the wider Glen Eden area.
