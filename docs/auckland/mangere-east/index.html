Quick answers

What is the population of Māngere East?

Māngere East had 2,637 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 2.1% from 2,583 in 2018.

What is the median income in Māngere East?

The median personal income in Māngere East is $37,800 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $44,800 across Auckland.

How much is rent in Māngere East?

Households renting in Māngere East paid a median of $550 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $660 a week.

What is the median age in Māngere East?

The median age in Māngere East is 32.4 years (2023 Census).
