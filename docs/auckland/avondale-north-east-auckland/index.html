Avondale North East (Auckland) population and demographics

Avondale North East (Auckland) is a statistical area in Whau Local Board, Auckland. At the 2023 Census it had 2,403 usually resident people (+8.1% since 2018). It is part of the wider Avondale (Auckland) area.
