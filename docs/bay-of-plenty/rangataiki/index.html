Quick answers

What is the population of Rangataiki?

Rangataiki had 123 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 6.8% from 132 in 2018.

What is the median income in Rangataiki?

The median personal income in Rangataiki is $60,900 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $39,900 across Bay of Plenty.

How much is rent in Rangataiki?

Households renting in Rangataiki paid a median of $150 a week at the 2023 Census.
