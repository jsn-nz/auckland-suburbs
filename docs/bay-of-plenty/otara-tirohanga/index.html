Ōtara-Tirohanga population and demographics

Ōtara-Tirohanga is a statistical area in Ōpōtiki District, Bay of Plenty. At the 2023 Census it had 1,407 usually resident people (+19.6% since 2018). It is part of the wider Ōpōtiki District Rural area.
