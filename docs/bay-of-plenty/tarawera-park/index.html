Quick answers

What is the population of Tarawera Park?

Tarawera Park had 1,530 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 3.2% from 1,482 in 2018.

What is the median income in Tarawera Park?

The median personal income in Tarawera Park is $28,300 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $39,900 across Bay of Plenty.

How much is rent in Tarawera Park?

Households renting in Tarawera Park paid a median of $330 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $550 a week.

What is the median age in Tarawera Park?

The median age in Tarawera Park is 40.8 years (2023 Census).
