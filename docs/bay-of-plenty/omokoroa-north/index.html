Quick answers

What is the population of Ōmokoroa North?

Ōmokoroa North had 4,371 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 36.2% from 3,210 in 2018.

What is the median income in Ōmokoroa North?

The median personal income in Ōmokoroa North is $35,500 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $39,900 across Bay of Plenty.

How much is rent in Ōmokoroa North?

Households renting in Ōmokoroa North paid a median of $580 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $700 a week.

What is the median age in Ōmokoroa North?

The median age in Ōmokoroa North is 54.7 years (2023 Census).
