Tihiotonga-Whakarewarewa population and demographics

Tihiotonga-Whakarewarewa is a statistical area in Rotorua District, Bay of Plenty. At the 2023 Census it had 873 usually resident people (+13.2% since 2018). It is part of the wider Springfield area.
