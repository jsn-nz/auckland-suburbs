Quick answers

What is the population of Mount Holdsworth?

Mount Holdsworth had 2,037 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 15.3% from 1,767 in 2018.

What is the median income in Mount Holdsworth?

The median personal income in Mount Holdsworth is $42,300 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $48,400 across Wellington.

How much is rent in Mount Holdsworth?

Households renting in Mount Holdsworth paid a median of $300 a week at the 2023 Census.
