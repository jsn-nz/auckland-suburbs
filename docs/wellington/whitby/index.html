Quick answers

What is the population of Whitby?

Whitby had 2,904 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 2.0% from 2,847 in 2018.

What is the median income in Whitby?

The median personal income in Whitby is $56,400 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $48,400 across Wellington.

How much is rent in Whitby?

Households renting in Whitby paid a median of $650 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $715 a week.

What is the median age in Whitby?

The median age in Whitby is 41.5 years (2023 Census).
