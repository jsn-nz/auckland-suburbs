Quick answers

What is the population of Miramar South?

Miramar South had 3,381 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 3.7% from 3,510 in 2018.

What is the median income in Miramar South?

The median personal income in Miramar South is $47,900 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $48,400 across Wellington.

How much is rent in Miramar South?

Households renting in Miramar South paid a median of $600 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $750 a week.

What is the median age in Miramar South?

The median age in Miramar South is 38.9 years (2023 Census).
