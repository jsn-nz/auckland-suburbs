Quick answers

What is the population of Wallaceville?

Wallaceville had 2,397 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 0.4% from 2,388 in 2018.

What is the median income in Wallaceville?

The median personal income in Wallaceville is $46,300 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $48,400 across Wellington.

How much is rent in Wallaceville?

Households renting in Wallaceville paid a median of $450 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $540 a week.

What is the median age in Wallaceville?

The median age in Wallaceville is 38.6 years (2023 Census).
