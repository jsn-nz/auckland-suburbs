Quick answers

What is the population of Alicetown-Melling?

Alicetown-Melling had 2,694 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 3.5% from 2,793 in 2018.

What is the median income in Alicetown-Melling?

The median personal income in Alicetown-Melling is $53,700 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $48,400 across Wellington.

How much is rent in Alicetown-Melling?

Households renting in Alicetown-Melling paid a median of $444 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $580 a week.

What is the median age in Alicetown-Melling?

The median age in Alicetown-Melling is 36.9 years (2023 Census).
