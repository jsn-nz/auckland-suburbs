Quick answers

What is the population of Chatham Islands?

Chatham Islands had 612 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 7.7% from 663 in 2018.

What is the median income in Chatham Islands?

The median personal income in Chatham Islands is $46,600 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $46,600 across Chatham Islands.

How much is rent in Chatham Islands?

Households renting in Chatham Islands paid a median of $150 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Chatham Islands?

The median age in Chatham Islands is 44.0 years (2023 Census).
