Quick answers

What is the population of Waimate West?

Waimate West had 1,320 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 1.1% from 1,335 in 2018.

What is the median income in Waimate West?

The median personal income in Waimate West is $26,300 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $41,500 across Canterbury.

How much is rent in Waimate West?

Households renting in Waimate West paid a median of $320 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Waimate West?

The median age in Waimate West is 53.4 years (2023 Census).
