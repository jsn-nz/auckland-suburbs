Quick answers

What is the population of Pleasant Point?

Pleasant Point had 1,428 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 4.2% from 1,371 in 2018.

What is the median income in Pleasant Point?

The median personal income in Pleasant Point is $40,000 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $41,500 across Canterbury.

How much is rent in Pleasant Point?

Households renting in Pleasant Point paid a median of $330 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Pleasant Point?

The median age in Pleasant Point is 45.9 years (2023 Census).
