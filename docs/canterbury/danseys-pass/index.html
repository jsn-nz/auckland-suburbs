Quick answers

What is the population of Danseys Pass?

Danseys Pass had 993 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 0.6% from 987 in 2018.

What is the median income in Danseys Pass?

The median personal income in Danseys Pass is $38,600 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $41,500 across Canterbury.

How much is rent in Danseys Pass?

Households renting in Danseys Pass paid a median of $200 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $400 a week.

What is the median age in Danseys Pass?

The median age in Danseys Pass is 46.1 years (2023 Census).
