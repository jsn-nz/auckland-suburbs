Quick answers

What is the population of Kaikōura?

Kaikōura had 2,316 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 4.2% from 2,223 in 2018.

What is the median income in Kaikōura?

The median personal income in Kaikōura is $33,100 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $41,500 across Canterbury.

How much is rent in Kaikōura?

Households renting in Kaikōura paid a median of $300 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $390 a week.

What is the median age in Kaikōura?

The median age in Kaikōura is 53.5 years (2023 Census).
