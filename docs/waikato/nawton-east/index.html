Quick answers

What is the population of Nawton East?

Nawton East had 4,377 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 3.9% from 4,212 in 2018.

What is the median income in Nawton East?

The median personal income in Nawton East is $38,600 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $42,400 across Waikato.

How much is rent in Nawton East?

Households renting in Nawton East paid a median of $430 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $565 a week.

What is the median age in Nawton East?

The median age in Nawton East is 31.5 years (2023 Census).
