Quick answers

What is the population of Mangakino?

Mangakino had 822 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 1.1% from 831 in 2018.

What is the median income in Mangakino?

The median personal income in Mangakino is $26,000 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $42,400 across Waikato.

How much is rent in Mangakino?

Households renting in Mangakino paid a median of $250 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Mangakino?

The median age in Mangakino is 50.7 years (2023 Census).
