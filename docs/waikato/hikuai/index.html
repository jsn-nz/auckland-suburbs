Quick answers

What is the population of Hikuai?

Hikuai had 270 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 13.9% from 237 in 2018.

What is the median income in Hikuai?

The median personal income in Hikuai is $37,400 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $42,400 across Waikato.

How much is rent in Hikuai?

Households renting in Hikuai paid a median of $200 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Hikuai?

The median age in Hikuai is 45.5 years (2023 Census).
