Quick answers

What is the population of Ōtorohanga?

Ōtorohanga had 3,180 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 5.1% from 3,027 in 2018.

What is the median income in Ōtorohanga?

The median personal income in Ōtorohanga is $34,900 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $42,400 across Waikato.

How much is rent in Ōtorohanga?

Households renting in Ōtorohanga paid a median of $340 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $520 a week.

What is the median age in Ōtorohanga?

The median age in Ōtorohanga is 36.8 years (2023 Census).
