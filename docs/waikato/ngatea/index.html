Quick answers

What is the population of Ngatea?

Ngatea had 1,548 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 7.7% from 1,437 in 2018.

What is the median income in Ngatea?

The median personal income in Ngatea is $36,600 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $42,400 across Waikato.

How much is rent in Ngatea?

Households renting in Ngatea paid a median of $366 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Ngatea?

The median age in Ngatea is 47.0 years (2023 Census).
