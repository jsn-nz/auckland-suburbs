Quick answers

What is the population of Tamahere South?

Tamahere South had 2,289 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 16.0% from 1,974 in 2018.

What is the median income in Tamahere South?

The median personal income in Tamahere South is $50,600 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $42,400 across Waikato.

How much is rent in Tamahere South?

Households renting in Tamahere South paid a median of $445 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Tamahere South?

The median age in Tamahere South is 48.2 years (2023 Census).
