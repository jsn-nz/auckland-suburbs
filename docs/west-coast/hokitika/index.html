Quick answers

What is the population of Hokitika?

Hokitika had 3,267 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 5.5% from 3,096 in 2018.

What is the median income in Hokitika?

The median personal income in Hokitika is $35,900 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $32,200 across West Coast.

How much is rent in Hokitika?

Households renting in Hokitika paid a median of $285 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $500 a week.

What is the median age in Hokitika?

The median age in Hokitika is 46.7 years (2023 Census).
