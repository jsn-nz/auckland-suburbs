Quick answers

What is the population of Seddon Park?

Seddon Park had 3,036 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 5.9% from 2,868 in 2018.

What is the median income in Seddon Park?

The median personal income in Seddon Park is $30,800 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $41,300 across Otago.

How much is rent in Seddon Park?

Households renting in Seddon Park paid a median of $220 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $610 a week.

What is the median age in Seddon Park?

The median age in Seddon Park is 45.4 years (2023 Census).
