Quick answers

What is the population of Warren Park?

Warren Park had 1,356 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 8.7% from 1,485 in 2018.

What is the median income in Warren Park?

The median personal income in Warren Park is $45,300 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $41,300 across Otago.

How much is rent in Warren Park?

Households renting in Warren Park paid a median of $550 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $300 a week.

What is the median age in Warren Park?

The median age in Warren Park is 30.7 years (2023 Census).
