Quick answers

What is the population of Alexandra North?

Alexandra North had 2,910 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 1.9% from 2,856 in 2018.

What is the median income in Alexandra North?

The median personal income in Alexandra North is $38,300 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $41,300 across Otago.

How much is rent in Alexandra North?

Households renting in Alexandra North paid a median of $350 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $540 a week.

What is the median age in Alexandra North?

The median age in Alexandra North is 47.5 years (2023 Census).
