Quick answers

What is the population of St Leonards?

St Leonards had 2,610 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 1.4% from 2,646 in 2018.

What is the median income in St Leonards?

The median personal income in St Leonards is $41,600 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $39,600 across Hawke's Bay.

How much is rent in St Leonards?

Households renting in St Leonards paid a median of $380 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $520 a week.

What is the median age in St Leonards?

The median age in St Leonards is 36.1 years (2023 Census).
