Quick answers

What is the population of McLean Park?

McLean Park had 2,484 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 2.6% from 2,550 in 2018.

What is the median income in McLean Park?

The median personal income in McLean Park is $40,900 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $39,600 across Hawke's Bay.

How much is rent in McLean Park?

Households renting in McLean Park paid a median of $422 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $630 a week.

What is the median age in McLean Park?

The median age in McLean Park is 40.9 years (2023 Census).
