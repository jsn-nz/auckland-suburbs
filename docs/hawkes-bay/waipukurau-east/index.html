Quick answers

What is the population of Waipukurau East?

Waipukurau East had 1,962 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 5.0% from 1,869 in 2018.

What is the median income in Waipukurau East?

The median personal income in Waipukurau East is $38,500 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $39,600 across Hawke's Bay.

How much is rent in Waipukurau East?

Households renting in Waipukurau East paid a median of $350 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $590 a week.

What is the median age in Waipukurau East?

The median age in Waipukurau East is 45.3 years (2023 Census).
