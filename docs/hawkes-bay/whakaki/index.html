Quick answers

What is the population of Whakaki?

Whakaki had 687 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 1.8% from 675 in 2018.

What is the median income in Whakaki?

The median personal income in Whakaki is $31,300 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $39,600 across Hawke's Bay.

How much is rent in Whakaki?

Households renting in Whakaki paid a median of $200 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Whakaki?

The median age in Whakaki is 43.4 years (2023 Census).
