Quick answers

What is the population of Kaiteriteri-Rīwaka?

Kaiteriteri-Rīwaka had 1,992 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 12.5% from 1,770 in 2018.

What is the median income in Kaiteriteri-Rīwaka?

The median personal income in Kaiteriteri-Rīwaka is $34,400 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $37,600 across Tasman.

How much is rent in Kaiteriteri-Rīwaka?

Households renting in Kaiteriteri-Rīwaka paid a median of $320 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $570 a week.

What is the median age in Kaiteriteri-Rīwaka?

The median age in Kaiteriteri-Rīwaka is 47.1 years (2023 Census).
