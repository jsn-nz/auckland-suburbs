Quick answers

What is the population of Marlborough Sounds East?

Marlborough Sounds East had 2,028 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 9.9% from 1,845 in 2018.

What is the median income in Marlborough Sounds East?

The median personal income in Marlborough Sounds East is $33,800 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $38,600 across Marlborough.

How much is rent in Marlborough Sounds East?

Households renting in Marlborough Sounds East paid a median of $200 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Marlborough Sounds East?

The median age in Marlborough Sounds East is 57.7 years (2023 Census).
