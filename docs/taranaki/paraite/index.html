Quick answers

What is the population of Paraite?

Paraite had 996 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 11.0% from 897 in 2018.

What is the median income in Paraite?

The median personal income in Paraite is $45,000 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $38,500 across Taranaki.

How much is rent in Paraite?

Households renting in Paraite paid a median of $393 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Paraite?

The median age in Paraite is 45.3 years (2023 Census).
