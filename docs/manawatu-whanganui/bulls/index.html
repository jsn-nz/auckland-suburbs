Quick answers

What is the population of Bulls?

Bulls had 2,055 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 6.2% from 1,935 in 2018.

What is the median income in Bulls?

The median personal income in Bulls is $43,600 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $36,700 across Manawatū-Whanganui.

How much is rent in Bulls?

Households renting in Bulls paid a median of $320 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $465 a week.

What is the median age in Bulls?

The median age in Bulls is 32.8 years (2023 Census).
