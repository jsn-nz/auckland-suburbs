Quick answers

What is the population of Waiōuru?

Waiōuru had 861 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 12.5% from 765 in 2018.

What is the median income in Waiōuru?

The median personal income in Waiōuru is $50,800 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $36,700 across Manawatū-Whanganui.

How much is rent in Waiōuru?

Households renting in Waiōuru paid a median of $170 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Waiōuru?

The median age in Waiōuru is 27.2 years (2023 Census).
