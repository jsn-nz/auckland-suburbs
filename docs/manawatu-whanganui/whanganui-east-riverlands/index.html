Whanganui East-Riverlands population and demographics

Whanganui East-Riverlands is a statistical area in Whanganui District, Manawatū-Whanganui. At the 2023 Census it had 2,208 usually resident people (+1.1% since 2018). It is part of the wider Whanganui East area.
