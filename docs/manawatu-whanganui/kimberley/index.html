Quick answers

What is the population of Kimberley?

Kimberley had 489 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 9.4% from 447 in 2018.

What is the median income in Kimberley?

The median personal income in Kimberley is $34,800 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $36,700 across Manawatū-Whanganui.

How much is rent in Kimberley?

Households renting in Kimberley paid a median of $360 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Kimberley?

The median age in Kimberley is 43.5 years (2023 Census).
