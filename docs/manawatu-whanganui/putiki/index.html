Quick answers

What is the population of Putiki?

Putiki had 741 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 11.3% from 666 in 2018.

What is the median income in Putiki?

The median personal income in Putiki is $41,900 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $36,700 across Manawatū-Whanganui.

How much is rent in Putiki?

Households renting in Putiki paid a median of $200 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Putiki?

The median age in Putiki is 50.1 years (2023 Census).
