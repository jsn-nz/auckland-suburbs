Royal Oak (Palmerston North City) population and demographics

Royal Oak (Palmerston North City) is a statistical area in Palmerston North City, Manawatū-Whanganui. At the 2023 Census it had 2,802 usually resident people (+19.3% since 2018). It is part of the wider Kelvin Grove area.
