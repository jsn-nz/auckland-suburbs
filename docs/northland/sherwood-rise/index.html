Quick answers

What is the population of Sherwood Rise?

Sherwood Rise had 2,424 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, down 1.3% from 2,457 in 2018.

What is the median income in Sherwood Rise?

The median personal income in Sherwood Rise is $37,200 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $34,100 across Northland.

How much is rent in Sherwood Rise?

Households renting in Sherwood Rise paid a median of $418 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $610 a week.

What is the median age in Sherwood Rise?

The median age in Sherwood Rise is 37.2 years (2023 Census).
