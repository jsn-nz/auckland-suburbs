Quick answers

What is the population of Kaiwaka?

Kaiwaka had 2,532 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 18.4% from 2,139 in 2018.

What is the median income in Kaiwaka?

The median personal income in Kaiwaka is $32,700 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $34,100 across Northland.

How much is rent in Kaiwaka?

Households renting in Kaiwaka paid a median of $380 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $600 a week.

What is the median age in Kaiwaka?

The median age in Kaiwaka is 43.6 years (2023 Census).
