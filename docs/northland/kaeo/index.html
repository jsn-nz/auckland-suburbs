Quick answers

What is the population of Kāeo?

Kāeo had 1,299 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 9.1% from 1,191 in 2018.

What is the median income in Kāeo?

The median personal income in Kāeo is $27,800 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $34,100 across Northland.

How much is rent in Kāeo?

Households renting in Kāeo paid a median of $270 a week at the 2023 Census.

What is the median age in Kāeo?

The median age in Kāeo is 44.3 years (2023 Census).
