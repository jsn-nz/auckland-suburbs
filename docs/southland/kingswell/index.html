Quick answers

What is the population of Kingswell?

Kingswell had 2,280 usually resident people at the 2023 Census, up 2.2% from 2,232 in 2018.

What is the median income in Kingswell?

The median personal income in Kingswell is $40,600 a year (2023 Census, people aged 15 and over), compared with $40,900 across Southland.

How much is rent in Kingswell?

Households renting in Kingswell paid a median of $360 a week at the 2023 Census. Newly lodged bonds more recently had a median rent of $520 a week.

What is the median age in Kingswell?

The median age in Kingswell is 37.1 years (2023 Census).
